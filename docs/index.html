ConvMamSPN + UGGP-ST

Mamba-based Spacecraft 6D Pose Estimation with Uncertainty-Guided Geometric Pseudo-label Self-Training

Zeng Xin et al.

Abstract

We present a unified framework for spacecraft 6D pose estimation and synthetic-to-real adaptation. ConvMamSPN predicts keypoint heatmaps from spacecraft images using a ConvNeXt backbone and a Mamba-based hierarchical decoder, then recovers 6D pose with geometric PnP. UGGP-ST improves target-domain generalization by teacher-student self-training with uncertainty-guided geometric consistency.

Method Overview

ConvMamSPN extracts hierarchical features and predicts keypoint heatmaps, then solves pose with PnP. UGGP-ST performs online teacher pseudo-label supervision for robust domain adaptation.

ConvMamSPN

UGGP-ST
